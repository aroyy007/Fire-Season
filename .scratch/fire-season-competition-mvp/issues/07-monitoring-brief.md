# 07: Create a Monitoring Brief from selected evidence

**What to build:** Generate a one-page Monitoring Brief from the selected Analysis Artifact month. The brief gives a Monitoring Analyst a printable summary of the region, seasonal context, native and comparable evidence, support, uncertainty, Investigation Priority, limitations, and Evidence Receipt ID.

**Blocked by:** 03: Inspect a month and trace its evidence; 06: Inspect 10 km blocks and Investigation Priorities.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The brief is generated from the same artifact fields shown on screen.
- [ ] It contains region, period, typical months, selected anomaly, native/comparable status, support, uncertainty, block priorities, and limits.
- [ ] An unavailable comparison is stated plainly without inventing a value.
- [ ] The brief fits one printable page at the supported paper sizes.
- [ ] The receipt ID and source attribution are present.
