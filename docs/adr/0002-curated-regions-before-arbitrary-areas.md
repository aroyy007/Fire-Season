# Curated regions before arbitrary areas

The MVP will offer one Science Pilot and one qualified Local Impact Case rather than implying that Comparable Activity is valid for every user-drawn polygon. Arbitrary area selection is valuable, but it can place the transfer outside its evaluated geography, land-cover mix, activity range, or observation support. Curated regions trade breadth for defensible scope; arbitrary polygons may return later only with explicit calibration-eligibility checks.
