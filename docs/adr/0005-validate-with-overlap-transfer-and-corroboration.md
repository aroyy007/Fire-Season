# Validate with overlap, geographic transfer, and corroboration

The primary quantitative test will use held-out MODIS–VIIRS overlap data, followed by a geographically separate Transfer Test Region. Burned-area imagery and incident records may provide Corroborating Evidence but will not be described as perfect ground truth for active-fire detections. This separates measurable transfer performance from contextual plausibility and prevents circular or overstated validation claims.
