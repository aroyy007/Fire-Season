# Fire Season

Fire Season is a decision-support product for interpreting historical satellite-detected burning activity across the MODIS and VIIRS eras. It exists to help analysts separate changes in recorded burning from changes in the observing system.

## People and decisions

**Monitoring Analyst**:
A district or regional land- or forest-management analyst who prepares seasonal monitoring plans and decides where further investigation is justified.
_Avoid_: Emergency responder, dispatcher, general user

**Monitoring Brief**:
A concise, evidence-backed summary of typical burning periods, unusual recorded activity, comparison credibility, and areas needing further investigation.
_Avoid_: Emergency alert, fire forecast, evacuation advice

**Investigation Priority**:
A recommendation to give a declared month and analysis area additional human review, field coordination, or monitoring attention.
_Avoid_: Emergency severity, dispatch priority, automatic action

## Measurements

**Native Sensor Record**:
Detected activity expressed on one source product's own measurement scale, with its platform and product identity preserved.
_Avoid_: Ground truth, true fire count

**Detected Activity Rate**:
Detected active land-cell-days relative to valid observed land-cell-days for a declared product, area, and period.
_Avoid_: Number of fires, burned area, fire risk

**Harmonization**:
An evaluated transfer that expresses one sensor product's detected activity on a declared reference product scale within a documented scope.
_Avoid_: Merging hotspots, adding sensor counts, universal correction

**Comparable Activity**:
The user-facing estimate produced by a released harmonization, representing what the declared reference product would be expected to record.
_Avoid_: Actual fires, fire probability, raw combined count

**Observation Support**:
The valid land-cell-day evidence available for a measurement after applying the declared quality policy.
_Avoid_: Coverage percentage without a stated denominator

**Unavailable Comparison**:
An explicit result state used when observation support, overlap, calibration evidence, or transfer scope is insufficient for Comparable Activity.
_Avoid_: Zero activity, no fire, safe

**Activity Anomaly**:
A descriptive comparison with the same calendar period in a fixed historical reference window.
_Avoid_: Forecast, warning, causal attribution

## Evidence and release

**Evidence Receipt**:
The reproducible record of an analysis's area, period, sources, exclusions, measurement policy, calibration release, uncertainty, and limitations.
_Avoid_: AI explanation, narrative summary

**Analysis Artifact**:
An immutable, versioned result generated by the scientific pipeline and consumed by the application without recalculating the analysis during the user session.
_Avoid_: Live model response, editable result, dashboard cache

**Open Resource**:
A public dataset, API, software package, or asset that the team can lawfully use without payment; free registration or a free access key is allowed when it does not create a paid dependency.
_Avoid_: Free trial, promotional credit, paid-tier dependency

**Calibration Release**:
A versioned harmonization that passed the declared evaluation and scope gates and is eligible to produce Comparable Activity.
_Avoid_: Trained model, experimental fit

**Reference Product**:
The declared satellite product scale on which Comparable Activity is expressed; the first release uses Aqua MODIS rather than an abstract true-fire scale.
_Avoid_: Ground truth sensor, truth scale

**Corroborating Evidence**:
Burned-area imagery, incident records, or other independent context used to interpret a result without treating it as perfect ground truth for active-fire detection.
_Avoid_: Validation target, definitive fire record

**Science Pilot**:
A data-rich study area used to establish whether the harmonization method is scientifically viable.
_Avoid_: Local impact claim

**Local Impact Case**:
A Chattogram- or Bangladesh-facing study area used only when its observation support and calibration scope justify a trustworthy result.
_Avoid_: Science pilot by default

**Curated Region**:
A predefined study area whose data availability, observation support, and calibration eligibility have been checked before release.
_Avoid_: Arbitrary user polygon, globally supported area

**Transfer Test Region**:
A geographically separate study area used to test whether a calibration remains credible outside its fitting geography.
_Avoid_: Second training region, local impact case by default

**Competition MVP**:
The offline-capable judged experience comprising two Curated Regions, the Burning Activity Calendar, Native Sensor Records, eligible Comparable Activity, evidence inspection, and a Monitoring Brief.
_Avoid_: Production service, global fire platform
